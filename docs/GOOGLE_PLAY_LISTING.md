# Google Play Listing Draft

**Title:** Don't Touch Red

**Short description:** Tap red orbs, avoid every other color, and chase your best score.

**Full description:** Don't Touch Red is an offline reflex game with one simple rule: red is safe. Tap red orbs before they disappear, avoid blue and yellow, build a combo, and survive as the pace increases. Earn coins from your runs, unlock red orb skins, buy shield and slow power-ups, complete missions and a daily challenge, and track your local records. Sound and vibration can be turned off in Settings.

**Features:** Fast touch gameplay, combos, increasing difficulty, lives, coins, skins, power-ups, missions, a daily challenge, achievements, statistics, and an offline local leaderboard.

**Category suggestion:** Games → Arcade. **Keywords:** reflex, tap, arcade, offline, score, combo.

**Privacy information required before publishing:** Confirm the final Android permissions and data collection behavior; host a privacy policy URL and complete Google Play's Data safety form. Current gameplay stores progress only on-device. Future ads, purchases, analytics, or cloud services would change the disclosure.

**Content rating:** Complete the Google Play questionnaire based on the final build. Current gameplay depicts abstract colored circles and no violence, but only the Play questionnaire determines the rating.

The listing must be reviewed against the actual release build. Online leaderboards, ads, purchases, and cloud saves are omitted because they are not implemented.
